Interpretation and limits
-------------------------
- Cold means empty thumbnail cache, not empty kernel/client/server caches.
- Metrics use compositor frame callbacks, not physical display presentation.
- Runs are sequential, not randomized repetitions on an otherwise idle host.
- MiB = 1,048,576 bytes. Allocator-control tables explicitly use decimal MB.
- The free-chunk counter describes allocator address space; some pages may have
  been returned even while that counter remains large. Read RSS alongside it.
- PSS apportions shared pages; summed process RSS can double-count libraries.
- The explicit trim is diagnostic, not a production fix. Active rescan work
  resumed allocating and regrew RSS after trimming.
- Historical inode mismatch is observed; its cause is not established. Fresh
  SSHFS scans and a short repeated-stat check showed stable current inodes.
- The forced refresh loop is a stress workload. The separate native-rescan case
  demonstrates substantial retention without that explicit loop.
