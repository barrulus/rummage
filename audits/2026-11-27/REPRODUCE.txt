NATIVE UI / PERFORMANCE

build's archives/flags. ui/seed_photo.py uses catalog/audit_support.py to prepare real
schema with synthetic rows. ui/run_harness.py sets isolated XDG roots, software GL and
a 120-second timeout. Preserve its fixture/data layout when reproducing. No-cursor
selection and bulk-property operations have ONE measured sample per size; normal
focused/draw operations use 30 samples and direct-view draw uses 60 after warmup.

CSV p95 for a one-sample operation is not a distribution claim. The generic collection
fixture and backend-backed photo fixture are different; avoid subtracting their costs
as if this were a profiler trace. Screenshot conversion helper is ui/ppm_png.py.
