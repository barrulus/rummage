Accounting:
- MiB uses 1,048,576 bytes; Heaptrack's MB/KB values are its decimal output.
- glibc free chunks are allocator address space, not necessarily resident bytes.
- RSS sums double-count shared pages across helpers; process-tree PSS is supplied.
- Texture budget estimates are not additional physical RAM to sum with RSS.
- One-second samples can miss brief peaks; helper/GPU allocation traces are not
  included in the parent Heaptrack profile.
