Before you reach for your wallet

A few entirely
reasonable questions.

Same application. Same features.
Three opportunities to download it.

What changes between the plans?
The heading, the joke, and one border. Every button takes you to the same downloads.
Where is the paid upgrade?
There isn't one. Folder Insights, customisation, and the preview helpers are part of rummage. Some previews need an optional helper package; those are free too.
Can I use it at work?
Yes. Rummage is MIT licensed, including for commercial use. See the licence for its terms and warranty disclaimer.
Does the department get a support contract?
No contract is included. These are development builds of an open-source project. Visit Support & community for help and ways to report a problem.
How can I help?
Report a reproducible bug, improve a guide, help with a translation, or contribute code. The contribution guide is a good place to start.

With thanks to our friends at Hesiod for inspiring this page. Good ideas are worth a rummage.
