Make a useful bug report

Help us see
what you're seeing.

Include these details in your GitHub issue:

  • Your rummage version, source commit, and installation method. rummage --about helps identify the build.
  • Your Linux distribution and desktop or compositor.
  • The steps to reproduce the problem, what you expected, and what happened.
  • A relevant screenshot or log excerpt, with personal information removed.

Development builds can share a version number, so the commit and any local changes matter.
