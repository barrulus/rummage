A few things before you start.

The Debian build targets Debian 13; it is not validated for Ubuntu. The Fedora RPM targets Fedora 45; use the separate Tumbleweed RPM for openSUSE.

These are individual packages, not an automatic-update repository. For a fixed version, keep the source commit recorded with your download.
